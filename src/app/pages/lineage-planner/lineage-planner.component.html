Affinity & inheritance guide

Uma Musume affinity calculator

Affinity, also called compatibility, affects the chance that factors activate during inheritance. Build a complete family tree above to compare your trainee with both parents, grandparents, and great-grandparents. The planner combines character compatibility, shared race affinity, and spark data in one place.

How to calculate compatibility

Select the target Uma first, then add two parents and their ancestors. The calculated affinity updates as you fill the lineage. Add each character's race wins and sparks for a more complete inheritance plan.

How to improve affinity

Start with compatible parents, then look for useful G1 wins shared across the family tree. The Optimal Races action identifies race overlap for the lineage you have selected.

What the planner shows

Review total and per-branch affinity, race contributions, base and per-run spark proc odds, combined spark chances, and potential white-spark inheritance before starting a training run.

Uma Musume affinity FAQ

What is affinity in Uma Musume?

Affinity is the compatibility score between a trainee and her inheritance characters. Higher affinity improves the chance that inherited factors activate.

How is Uma Musume compatibility calculated?

Select the trainee and her lineage. The calculator combines character compatibility with applicable shared-race bonuses throughout the family tree.

How can I improve affinity?

Choose compatible inheritance characters and plan overlapping race wins across the family tree. The Optimal Races action identifies useful shared G1 wins for the selected lineage.

How can I find a parent with the sparks I need?

Use the Uma Musume trainer and parent database to filter inheritance records by character, support card, factors, sparks, race wins, and affinity.
